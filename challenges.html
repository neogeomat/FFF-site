Challenges

Challenges that open new paths

What remains to scale producer-led forest management — financing, policy and inclusion.

01

Scaling

From 68 organizations to provincial coverage. Federations must carry peer learning and services after FFF. Investment in second-tier bodies is next.

  • Replica grants via federations
  • Youth entrepreneurship
  • South-South exchange
02

Financing

Beyond grants — blended finance. De-risking loans for producer organizations, buyer pre-finance and payment for ecosystem services.

  • Credit + grants packages
  • Buyer contracts as collateral
  • Climate finance (REDD+, carbon)
03

Forest Policy

Secure tenure and simple rules for community forestry. 46 local plans influenced — national policy still lags on permits and incentives.

  • Tenure clarification
  • Simplified permits
  • Incentives for restoration

Actions ahead

FFF’s next phase: strengthen federations as service providers, link organizations to climate and value-chain finance, and embed community forestry in municipal land planning. Progress is tracked in Progress and products in Catalog.
